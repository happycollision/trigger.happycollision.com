The lyrics tool supports offline mode. Just wait a while and its pages should be loaded and ready to go, even if you lose your internet connection.

You may need to add this site to your home screen to get it to work offline.

In Ear is a listening lab for learning monitor setups. It requires an internet connection; music loads only when you press Play and is not included in the lyrics tool’s offline downloads.
